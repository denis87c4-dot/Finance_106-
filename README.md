# Finance 106 - Financial Manager

Professional financial management and quantitative cash flow dashboard suite, migrated to high-performance React + TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.

## Features

- **📊 Dashboard Executivo**: Overall cash flow summary, budget vs. realized tracking by category, monthly revenue vs expense breakdown, and expense share distribution.
- **📊 Distribuição Normal & Probabilidades (Norm. Dist.)**: Gaussian distribution analysis for Income, Expense, Cash Flow, and Cumulative balance with interactive cutoff threshold ($X$), theoretical PDF/CDF bell curve with shaded probability integrals.
- **⚡ Advanced KPIs 2**: 20 quantitative and econometric indicators including Historical VaR (95%), CVaR, Expense Coverage (ICD), Burn Rate Volatility, Tail Share, Resilience Factor, Rel. IQR, Tail Ratio, HHI, and Synthetic Stress Indicator.
- **🚀 Advanced Analytics & Statistical KPIs**: Coefficient of Variation (CV), Median Absolute Deviation (MAD), P95 tail percentile, Shannon Entropy, Skewness, Kurtosis, Gini index, and empirical frequency histogram.
- **🔍 Auditoria Avançada**: Statistical anomaly and outlier detection ($> \mu + 2\sigma$), critical pending item tracker, and filtered compliance logs.
- **📈 Econometria & Estatística (Statistic 2)**: Geometric and harmonic means, trimmed means, semi-standard deviation, personal Sharpe & Sortino ratios, Bollinger Bands, OLS linear trend regression, Pareto ABC classification, and capital drawdown curves.
- **📊 Gráficos Sofisticados**: Financial health index score (0-100), operational runway in months, and cumulative wealth timeline.
- **📋 Central de Lançamentos**: Full transaction management with instant search, multi-parameter filtering, inline editing, deletion, and CSV export.
- **➕ Novo Cadastro**: Income, expense, and transfer registration with installment support (up to 24x) and cash vs. credit terms.
- **🏷️ Categorias & Contas**: Flexible taxonomy customization for financial classifications and bank accounts.
- **💳 Cartões de Crédito**: Credit limit monitoring, closing dates, due dates, and utilized credit tracking.
- **🤖 IA & Assistente**: Predictive diagnostic report with automated heuristics and interactive financial query advisor.
- **🛡️ Backup & Segurança**: Full JSON state backups, CSV imports/exports, and demo data restoration.
